Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

ምእራፍ ("we", "the site") is a small, independent platform for sharing short stories. This policy explains what information we collect and how it's used.

What we collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (handled securely through Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password directly). If you upload a book, we store the title, description, genre, cover image, and PDF file you provide.

How we use it

Your information is used only to operate the site: to let you log in, to attribute books to their authors, and to let other visitors browse and download approved uploads. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for advertising.

Where your data lives

Account data and book information are stored with Firebase (Google). Cover images and PDF files are stored with Cloudinary. Both are reputable third-party services used only to run the site's core features.

Uploaded content

Every book uploaded is reviewed by the site admin before it appears publicly. We reserve the right to remove any content that violates our Terms of Service.

Your choices

You can delete your own uploaded books at any time from within the site. If you'd like your account or data removed entirely, contact us using the email below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to eleroidbussines@gmail.com.
